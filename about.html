Our Story

Driven by quality, powered by trust.

Founded in 1962, Punjab Auto Stores and Punjab Trading Company have built a long-standing position in Pakistan's heavy-vehicle spare-parts market. The business specializes in Japanese trucks including Hino, Isuzu, UD, Mazda and Bedford.

Our focus is straightforward: dependable parts, competitive pricing, professional support and a supply network that helps customers keep commercial vehicles on the road.

Vision
